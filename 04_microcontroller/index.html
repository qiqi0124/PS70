<div class="textcontainer"> <p class="margin"> </p> <h3>Week 4: Microcontroller Programming</h3> <p class="margin"> </p> <h4>Light Detection Circuit</h4> <p class="margin"> </p> <p class="margin"> </p> This week's task was to program a microcontroller to do something. As someone who is always getting yelled at by my parents for working in the dark, I thought it would be cool to build a light detecting circuit. Watch the video below for the final product! <p class="margin"> </p> <p class="margin"> </p> <video width="400" height="300" controls> <source src="ldrvid.mp4" type="video/mp4"> Your browser does not support the video tag. </video> <p class="margin"> </p> <p class="margin"> </p> To build this circuit, I used an ESP32 microcontroller, three 1k ohm resistors, one GL5516 photoresistor, one 220 ohm resistor, and 3 leds (red, yellow, and green). When the circuit detects low lighting, only the red LED will be on. As brightness increases to a mid-range level, the yellow LED will then also light up. At the desired brightness, all three leds, including the green LED, will be on. <p class="margin"> </p> <p class="margin"> </p> <image style = "width: 50%"src = "ldr_circuit.jpg" alt = "ldrcircuit"> </image> <p class="margin"> </p> <p class="margin"> </p> The microcontroller was able to take in the changing resistance of the photoresistor and depending on the value, offer power to each of the LEDs.
